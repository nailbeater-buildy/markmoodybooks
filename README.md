# Mark Moody Books — Author Website

Official one-page website for author and Bible teacher Rev. Mark L. Moody, designed for thoughtful evangelical Christians, Bible study groups, and church leaders looking for scripture-rich biblical investigations.

## Books & Resources Featured
- **Communion's Forgotten Story: Reclaiming What We Lost**
- **Communion's Forgotten Story Study Guide: A Small Group Companion** (Free 12-session PDF)
- **Sincerely Mistaken: The Sabbath-Sunday Question**
- **The Sabbath Exposed: Unexpected Discoveries, Compelling Evidence. Does the Fourth Commandment apply to new covenant believers?**

## The Mission
All author royalties from *Communion's Forgotten Story* support the missionary work of Dr. Matthew and Amy-Lynn Ness at Rift Valley Academy in Kijabe, Kenya.
