Communion's Forgotten Story

Reclaiming What We Lost

We take the bread. We drink the cup. We remember Jesus. But for many of us, that's where the understanding stops. What if there's far more to Communion than we ever realized?

Communion's Forgotten Story traces the Lord's Supper from its roots in Eden, through the Passover and the upper room, through the early church and into your hands today. Along the way, you'll discover that when Jesus broke the bread and lifted the cup, He wasn't inventing a new ritual — He was fulfilling a story woven through Scripture from the very beginning. A story of promise. Of deliverance. Of sacrifice. Of hope.

Written for Christians of every tradition — whether you call it Communion, the Eucharist, or the Lord's Supper — this book will change the way you come to the table. Not with less reverence, but with far greater understanding.

Every copy purchased supports the missionary work of Mark's daughter and son-in-law in Kijabe, Kenya. Read their story below.

Communion's Forgotten Story Study Guide

A twelve-session companion built to help small groups explore the Lord's Supper together — from its roots in Eden through the Passover, the upper room, and into the life of the early church.

Written by a pastor with forty-five years of cross-denominational ministry, it doesn't advocate for any one tradition or take sides on the debates that divide — ordinance or sacrament, symbolic or real presence, open table or closed. It simply invites every believer to recover the ancient story behind the meal they already share.

Each session runs about an hour: an icebreaker, Scripture to explore, discussion questions, personal reflection, and a closing prayer. No theological background required — just open Bibles and honest conversation.

Sincerely Mistaken

The Sabbath-Sunday Question

For most Christians, Sunday worship has never been a question worth asking. It was simply the way things were — received through tradition, affirmed by trusted teachers, rarely examined closely. Until one pastor, after forty-five years of ministry, finally sat down with Scripture intending to defend what he had always believed. What he discovered surprised him.

Sincerely Mistaken traces one man's honest investigation into one of Christianity's longest-standing assumptions — that the Sabbath was transferred from the seventh day to Sunday. Moving carefully through Scripture and two thousand years of church history, it asks a question most believers have never seriously considered: where, exactly, does Scripture authorize the change?

Written with humility and deep respect for sincere Christians on every side, this is not a book written to win an argument. It is an invitation to examine familiar assumptions honestly and consider the evidence for yourself. The door is open. Come and see.

About Mark

With over forty-five years in full-time evangelical ministry, Rev. Mark L. Moody brings the heart of a pastor and the clarity of a Bible teacher to every page.

Originally from Washington State, he spent much of his ministry in British Columbia, Canada, where he served as a youth pastor, devoted a decade to Teen Challenge, and pastored churches for more than thirty years. He now assists in leadership at a community church serving seasonal residents in the California desert.

Writing from within the historic Protestant tradition, he is convinced that sincere Christians should never be afraid to reexamine long-held assumptions in the light of God's Word.

Mark and his wife are the proud parents of four children and grandparents to fifteen grandchildren. He brings the same love for family to every page he writes.
