The Books

Book One

The Sabbath Exposed

Unexpected Discoveries, Compelling Evidence

Does the Fourth Commandment apply to new covenant believers? When challenged to defend the biblical basis of his chosen day of worship, Mark launched a wide-ranging, Scripture-rich investigation spanning Genesis to Revelation. The result is an invitation to step into that same journey through Scripture, history, and tradition — and decide for yourself, one revelation at a time.

Book Two

Communion's Forgotten Story

Reclaiming What We Lost

We take the bread. We drink the cup. We remember Jesus. But for many of us, that's where the understanding stops. This book traces the Lord's Supper from its roots in Eden, through the Passover and the upper room, through the early church and into your hands today — written for Christians of every tradition, whether you call it Communion, the Eucharist, or the Lord's Supper.

The Mission

“We have felt called to take steps towards returning to Africa as a family. The doors have now opened.” — Matt & Amy-Lynn Ness

All proceeds from Communion's Forgotten Story go directly to support Dr. Matthew Ness (Family Physician) and Amy-Lynn Ness (Elementary Education) and their three daughters, serving with Africa Inland Mission at Rift Valley Academy in Kijabe, Kenya — a school with over a century of ministry history, home to nearly 500 students from 30 countries, most of them children of missionary families serving across Africa.

After a life-changing three-month trip to Kenya in 2024, the Ness family felt an undeniable call to return. They began serving in Kijabe in July 2026.

About Mark

Rev. Mark L. Moody is a retired pastor, Bible teacher, and author with over 45 years in ministry. From his early years as Director of Teen Challenge — walking alongside people in their darkest moments — to decades of pastoral ministry across British Columbia, Canada, Mark has spent his life pointing people toward Scripture and lasting transformation.

With a Pentecostal background and an evangelical heart, he was known throughout BC for Scripture-driven preaching and gracious conviction. Originally from Washington State, Mark spent most of his ministry in BC. He writes the way he preached — with careful biblical scholarship, genuine humility, and a deep love for God's Word.

Mark and his wife are the proud parents of four children and grandparents to fifteen grandchildren. He brings the same love for family to every page he writes.
