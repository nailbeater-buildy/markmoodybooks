A Story with a Global Purpose

“We have felt called to take steps towards returning to Africa as a family. The doors have now opened.” — Dr. Matthew & Amy-Lynn Ness

All author proceeds from Communion's Forgotten Story and its study guide directly fund the missionary work of Mark's daughter and son-in-law, serving with Africa Inland Mission at Rift Valley Academy in Kijabe, Kenya.

About Rev. Mark L. Moody

“Writing from within the historic Protestant tradition, convinced that sincere Christians should never fear examining long-held assumptions in the light of Scripture.”

With over forty-five years in full-time pastoral ministry, Rev. Mark L. Moody brings both a shepherd's warmth and a scholar's rigor to every subject he touches. Originally from Washington State, he spent much of his active ministry in British Columbia, Canada — serving as a youth pastor, directing Teen Challenge for a decade, and pastoring local congregations for more than thirty years.

He currently assists in pastoral leadership at a desert community church in California. Mark and his wife are the blessed parents of four children and fifteen grandchildren.
